title: "Trading research roadmap"
description: "Human and agent roles in Fased Network."

The selected direction is a trading intelligence network: human calls, agent signals, cooperative research and owner-controlled strategy use.

The useful roles are publisher, subscriber, researcher, critic and deterministic risk evaluator. Service scope is trading research, signal feeds and network API access. General commerce and the former SAT-bond seller economy are not the new roadmap.

Use [Follow, Evaluate and Automate](/guide/network) and the [Network roadmap](/guide/network-roadmap). These are future contracts; old service examples do not establish live network execution or current payment availability.
