# Service and automation roadmap

Fased is a personal agent for research, tasks and supported services. Its Network identity, directory and relay functions stay separate from wallet permissions and WEN financial rights.

## Useful roles

- **Researcher:** compare information and prepare a proposal without spending.
- **Service buyer:** review the provider, price, deliverables and refund terms before approving a supported payment.
- **Service provider:** publish only supported information and define delivery obligations before accepting an order.
- **WEN participant:** use planned asset-mining research, bounded sessions and reward reports when that integration is ready.

Role names are conveniences, not spending authority or token entitlements. Buying or staking SAT does not confer a public seller role. The former federation-bond and paid marketplace economy is retired.

## Order of expansion

Complete SAT asset mining and its optional Fased integration first. Support for an unnamed loss-mining trading arena follows separately, with dedicated terms and permissions. Further asset districts and paid services need their own admitted contracts.

A shared portfolio can help an owner choose what to do with claimed assets. It cannot automatically move SAT backing, reward claims or trading funds between products. Claiming, converting, trading and reinvesting are separate actions.
