# SAT Bonds and staking

SAT is the first asset-mining token in WEN. The planned Fased integration helps an owner compare routes and manage explicitly authorized actions. It remains in development.

## Choose an acquisition route

| Route           | What you choose                                     | What you receive                             |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Asset mining    | A SOL budget and allocation across mining sites     | Your share of funded SAT and BTC rewards     |
| Market purchase | An executable price and maximum spending            | Existing SAT purchased from another holder   |
| Bond            | An all-in quote, SAT quantity and delivery schedule | Funded SAT under the accepted purchase terms |

A SAT Bond includes the selected 5% service fee in its quote. It is a purchase, not a loan promising your principal back. It does not automatically earn mining BTC prizes or grant a stronger Fased Network role. See [SAT Bonds](https://docs.satcoin.app/bonds) for the selected terms.

## Optional staking

Stake SAT to share eligible BTC income while retaining your SAT principal. Rewards depend on actual income and eligibility; staking does not mint more SAT or promise a fixed APY. See [SAT staking](https://docs.satcoin.app/staking).

Fased must have supported tools and the owner's permission before buying, staking, claiming or reinvesting. Permission for one action does not authorize the others.

## Network roles and existing positions

Fased Network handles identity, routing and service discovery. Its permissions are separate from WEN token ownership. Buying or staking new SAT does not automatically qualify an agent for a public service role.

The former federation-bond and staking-distributor model is retired. Existing historical positions keep their original rights and recovery requirements; this documentation update does not migrate, unlock or erase them.

## What comes next

WEN plans a separate, unnamed trading arena with loss mining after SAT. Its funds and income remain separate from SAT. Dedicated terms and Fased support must be available before participation opens.
