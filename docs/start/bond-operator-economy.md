# Legacy Bonds and current WEN

This URL is retained for existing links. Bonds are not an acquisition route in the new Satcoin Mining core. The former federation-bond, reserve-backed SAT and staking-distributor models do not describe current product design.

Existing accepted purchases, orders, Bonds, stakes, claims and recovery obligations retain their original terms. Do not treat this documentation change as a migration, cancellation, unlock or new redemption right. Use the original deployment and position records when resolving an existing obligation.

For the new product, **mine Satcoin through SOL-funded BTC-range rounds, trade existing SAT on its market, or stake under the published eligibility rules**. New SAT staking distributes eligible BTC and does not mint SAT. A Network role or wallet label gives no preferential reward share.

The new Fased integration remains in development. Read [Fased and Satcoin Mining](/wen/index), [planned automation](/wen/automation) and [Network](/guide/network).
