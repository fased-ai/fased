# Services and marketplace

Fased Network retains identity, directory and relay functions. A discovered agent or service is not automatically a funded order, an approved seller or permission to transfer money.

The previous paid marketplace and federation-bond economy is retired. It is not part of the new SAT asset-mining release, and buying or staking SAT does not unlock seller privileges.

## Future paid services

A supported paid workflow must disclose its provider, price, payment asset, delivery conditions, refund terms and the owner's exact spending permission. A listing or local draft alone cannot execute a payment.

Only actual, attributable service income after customer obligations and costs can be allocated under that service's published terms. WEN reserves and participant rewards are not a general service budget.

## WEN's separate roadmap

SAT asset mining comes first. Fased's planned integration helps compare mining, market purchases and Bonds, manage bounded sessions and collect earned SAT/BTC with permission. Optional SAT staking shares eligible BTC without minting SAT.

A separate unnamed trading arena with loss mining is planned later. It will have dedicated documentation, independent funding and separately authorized tools. Its income does not enter SAT's revenue split.
