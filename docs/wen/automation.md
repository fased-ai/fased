---
title: "WEN automation boundaries"
description: "Admitted products, separate permissions and recoverable execution."
---

The planned WEN desk can analyze opportunities, allocate campaign budgets, rebalance, stake and claim through individually admitted services. Subscriptions, Ventures, Notes, contests and borrowing require their own product gates; listing an action here does not enable it.

Each authority needs an owner, account/asset scope, spending limits, expiry, reconciliation and a revocation/recovery path. An uncertain transaction must be reconciled before another submission. Rebalancing and borrowing are not implied by permission to claim rewards.

An optional keeper uses its own RPC and fee-paying wallet. It processes program-permitted work without player private keys. Any eligible execution compensation needs published funding; it creates no preferred mining price or allocation.

User-owned strategies may change standing instructions within authority. Financial rules and custody remain on chain. Use [wallet policies](/plugins/crypto/wallet-roles-and-policies), [wallet setup](/plugins/crypto/wallet-page) and [WEN release status](https://docs.wen.network/status).
