WEN — World Economy Network — is a strategy game starting with asset mining. In Bitcoin District, players choose a SOL spending budget and mining allocation to earn funded SAT and BTC. SAT's USDC backing, retained BTC and participant rewards remain separate.

The WEN app is the ordinary way to participate. Fased is optional: a personal agent for deeper research and automation.

<Note>Fased's WEN integration is in development. The features below are planned, not instructions for an available release.</Note>

## The planned WEN desk

- Compare mining-site allocations, market buying and Bonds.
- Set per-round, daily and total spending limits, minimum awards and session expiry.
- Collect earned SAT/BTC, withdraw unused budgets and manage optional SAT staking for eligible BTC income.
- Explain spending, rewards and crowding; pause future entries or change an authorized strategy.

Fased does not receive preferential prices, mining allocations or rewards. Automation follows the same product rules and cannot guarantee returns.

SAT comes first. WEN plans a separate trading arena with loss mining next, with its own funding and reward rules and dedicated documentation before participation opens. Future trading actions need separate supported tools and owner permissions. A shared portfolio does not transfer assets or combine product reserves.

Read [WEN automation](/wen/automation) for permissions and limits. WEN's economic rules belong in its own documentation; this guide covers your agent.
