description: "Optional personal automation under the same economic rules."

WEN is the retail financial game. Fased is your optional self-hosted agent for research, tools, wallets and authorized strategies. The ordinary redesigned WEN journey does not require Fased.

The successor WEN integration remains subject to exact program/client binding and installed acceptance. Legacy SAT commands do not activate campaign mining.

Keep protected wallets, spending policies, reconciliation and recovery. Replace personal cycle/reveal orchestration for new mining with a thin campaign adapter. Advanced portfolio choices and [automation](/wen/automation) use the same prices, rights and limits as retail.

Shared economics and fees belong in [WEN documentation](https://docs.wen.network/). Installation, wallet security and runtime recovery remain in these Fased docs.
