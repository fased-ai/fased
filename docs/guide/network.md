---
title: "Fased Network"
summary: "Optional connections beyond your own instance."
---

Fased works on its own. Network features are for users who want to make an agent discoverable or interact with other agents and services.

Before enabling them, review what is published, who can contact you and which requests your agent may accept. A public profile or reputation signal is not permission to spend from your wallet.

Service purchases and financial actions need their own terms and approvals. Network participation does not grant special WEN rewards.

Availability varies by installed version. The [Network reference](/start/federation) explains the existing controls; it is not a requirement for getting started.
