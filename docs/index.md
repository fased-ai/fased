Fased is a personal agent you run on your own computer or server. Use it for research, tasks and connected tools through a browser control panel.

Start with one model and one task. Add channels, automation or wallets when you need them.

- [Get started](/start/getting-started): install, connect a model and send your first message.
- [Everyday use](/guide/everyday): work with conversations, files and scheduled tasks.
- [Tools](/guide/tools): choose the services your agent can use.
- [Wallet safety](/guide/wallets): understand permissions before enabling financial actions.

## Fased and WEN

WEN is Fcode Lab's Asset Mining platform. Its first product is **Satcoin Mining: Mine Satcoin. Compete for BTC.** Participants allocate SOL across BTC price ranges in ten-minute rounds; committed capital and accuracy determine shares of released BTC and unlocked SAT.

Fased's planned WEN integration adds optional research, bounded mining strategies and authorized reward management. It remains in development. You do not need Fased to use WEN, or WEN to use Fased. Fcode Lab is the umbrella behind both products; a shared brand does not pool their funds or permissions.

Read [Fased and WEN](/wen/index). For source code and releases, visit [GitHub](https://github.com/fased-ai/fased).
