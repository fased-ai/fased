WEN is an Asset Mining platform. Satcoin Mining allocates SOL across ten-minute BTC price ranges. Capital × accuracy determines shares of released fresh BTC and unlocked SAT. Satcoin is the asset name; SAT is the shorthand used for quantities. The new Fased integration remains in development.

The planned experience uses bounded sessions with per-round and total spending limits, minimum awards and expiry. Review results, claim rewards, optionally stake SAT for eligible BTC, or withdraw unused funds. SAT staking does not mint new SAT. Depositing a budget does not spend it all, and unused funds have no idle erosion.

WEN provides the ordinary participant experience. Fased is an optional personal agent for comparing opportunities and managing authorized strategies.

Exact-range entries return 99% of principal after the fee; incorrect entries return 90%–95% and may earn partial rewards. BTC, SAT and execution costs are shown separately.

There are no activation instructions on this page. Follow [Fased and WEN](/wen/index) for the current scope and [wallet safety](/guide/wallets) before enabling financial actions.
