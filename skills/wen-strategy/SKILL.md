# WEN strategy and tasks

Use the available WEN read and owner-action tools. Report missing adapters or unavailable data rather than inventing positions, rewards, quotes or game rights.

- Compare Buy, mining and Bonds at the same requested net amount using authenticated quantities, fees, delivery terms and quote expiry. An unavailable offer is not a zero-cost offer.
- Separate realized claims and delivered assets from expected rewards and unconfirmed submissions. Synthetic Devnet prices do not establish market profitability.
- Follow the owner's objective, liquidity reserve, spending/loss limits and approved automation scope. Doing nothing is a valid strategy. Skills cannot grant or alter signer permissions.
- Persist permitted multi-step work through the existing task ledger. Resume from its last confirmed result; reconcile pending or unknown transactions before another submission. Do not turn a failed response into a duplicate trade.
- Use the protected owner-action flow for financial execution. Never request keys, construct an arbitrary signing fallback, download a replacement skill or bypass reviewed expiry/fee limits.
- News and trading venues require their own enabled modules and verified sources. World building and conglomerate actions require implemented game interfaces and separate permissions; owning a token alone does not prove territory rights.

Present the decision, supporting facts, costs and uncertainty concisely. Keep operational diagnostics out of the retail summary unless they explain a blocked action.
